PASSAGE · RESEARCH SYNTHESIS · 2187

Designing for different levels of confidence, familiarity, and journey complexity.

These proto-personas were synthesized from spaceflight physical constraints, human-rated transport rules, and 2187 orbital assumptions. They directly informed interface language, recovery logic, and trust signals.

P1 · PRIMARY

Aarav Mehta

Systems Engineer · Frequent Traveller

“Show me the most reliable way there, what I do next, and what changes if something goes wrong.”
Age31
OriginIndia
Tech ComfortVery high
Travel StyleFrequent

Goals

  • Reach destinations reliably
  • Minimise avoidable delays
  • Keep onward transfers protected
  • Decision-critical tech detail only

Pain Points

  • Unclear transfer risk
  • Last-minute schedule changes
  • Operational jargon overload
  • Restarting route after disruption

Behaviour

  • Compares duration before cost
  • Checks spacecraft readiness
  • Accepts multi-hop if confident
  • Wants concise evidence updates

PASSAGE Needs

  • Journey-first recommendations
  • Protected connections
  • Data freshness indicators
  • Preserve viable onward legs
Journey Context Lunar Gateway → Europa Research Colony · approx. 96 days · 3 spacecraft · 2 transfers
P2 · SECONDARY

Maya Chen

Documentary Researcher · Infrequent Traveller

“Tell me where I’m going, what happens at each stop, and whether the connection is still safe.”
Age26
OriginEast Asia
Tech ComfortMedium–high
Travel StyleInfrequent

Goals

  • Understand full route upfront
  • Feel safe across unfamiliar hubs
  • Know what happens at transfers
  • Travel without orbital mechanics

Pain Points

  • Dense technical terminology
  • Unclear multi-leg routes
  • Uncertainty in ship conditions
  • Fear of missing a connection

Behaviour

  • Prefers guided recommendations
  • Relies on visual route maps
  • Reads safety details carefully
  • Needs reassurance after changes

PASSAGE Needs

  • Visual journey storytelling
  • Plain-language transfer status
  • Habitability & safety cues
  • Clear next physical action
Journey Context First long-haul assignment beyond Mars; prioritises clarity and trust over raw speed.
P3 · SITUATIONAL

Dr. Lena Ortiz

Medical Specialist · Multi-Hop Traveller

“Connections are fine. I just need the whole journey to keep working.”
Age42
OriginInternational
Tech ComfortHigh
Travel StyleMulti-hop

Goals

  • Reach remote sites predictably
  • Maintain viable onward legs
  • Understand medical capability
  • Reduce mission schedule shock

Pain Points

  • Single-leg changes breaking all
  • Unclear medical capability
  • Disconnected operators
  • Manual re-planning at gateways

Behaviour

  • Comfortable with multi-transfers
  • Reviews mission constraints
  • Accepts longer routes if resilient
  • Values operational transparency

PASSAGE Needs

  • Route continuity intelligence
  • Medical support indicators
  • Impact summaries on disruption
  • Cross-operator booking logic
Journey Context Relocation via Lunar, Mars and Jupiter gateways; high transfer tolerance, zero tolerance for full itinerary failure.
DESIGN TRANSLATION · WHAT THESE PERSONAS CHANGED
Aarav Mehta (Primary) Made reliability, protected transfers, and recovery impact first-class information on every card and itinerary view.
Maya Chen (Secondary) Pushed the interface toward visual routes, plain language status cues, and confidence-building habitability signals.
Dr. Lena Ortiz (Situational) Validated journey continuity as more critical than restarting search after one cancelled or rescheduled leg.
